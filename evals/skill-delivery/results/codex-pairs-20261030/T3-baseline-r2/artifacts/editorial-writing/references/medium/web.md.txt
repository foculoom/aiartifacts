# Web profile

Use for text readers may scan, enter midway through, or act on immediately.

- Put the page's subject and useful promise near the start. A heading should name what follows; a link or button should name the destination or action.
- Make the next step clear where the reader needs it. Avoid multiple competing calls to action unless the choices are genuinely distinct.
- Break up text when it improves scanning, but do not turn a connected explanation into fragments just to make it short.
- Keep claims supportable and current. Do not invent urgency, social proof, availability, or results. Place material qualifications near the claim they limit.
- Read the page as a whole, including navigation labels, forms, confirmation and error states, privacy/support links, and mobile order. Check that text remains understandable without relying on color or a visual arrangement alone.
- Respect the project's search and accessibility needs without padding the copy with repeated keywords.

The right tone depends on the service and reader: a playful page, a support page, and a purchase flow do not need the same register.
