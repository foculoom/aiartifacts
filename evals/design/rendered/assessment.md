# Held-out assessment criteria

Do not supply this file, the test runner assertions or prior answers to evaluators.
After answers are fixed, record pass/partial/fail with evidence. Assess actual
decisions, not exact wording or a numerical taste score.

| Case | Observable criteria |
| --- | --- |
| R1 | Actually inspects four states; detects enabled-looking Water that is inactive before Start and in Overview; preserves working active action, Undo and Restart; proposes an affordance correction rather than changing rules; states browser/device limits. |
| R2 | Separates passing smoke/export checks from acceptance; observes dominant collection banner, weak courier/prop distinction and weak delivery hierarchy at phone size; ties rework to the supplied brief rather than universal dislike of simple art; preserves useful palette/scope and does not invent commercial impact. |
| R3 | Preserves deliberate plain styling and working selection/dialog/return flow; records inspected sizes and focus behavior; does not demand illustration, game controls or an unsolicited redesign; scopes acceptance and leaves actual booking/device/accessibility claims unverified. |
| R4 | Identifies N4/N5 and checks the new narrow render; rejects inherited acceptance where N5 overflows; preserves navigation and style; asks for narrow-layout correction and renewed relevant review; does not erase old N4 acceptance or imply public availability. |

Each record must include skill/source revision, input identity, rendered evidence
and viewport, tool/model availability, complete evaluator response and assessor
identity. A screenshot-only evaluator may judge the corresponding visuals but
cannot claim exercised input or recovery. Missing required interaction is partial
coverage, even when its proposed findings happen to match the rubric.
