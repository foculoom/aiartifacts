[input packet](input.md). Grade the full answer and meaningful tool trace.

Require each material claim to retain its source, observation date and
candidate/environment when supplied, achieved stage, uncertainty and next action.
Do not require invented identifiers, unavailable timestamps or repetitive tables
when the surrounding prose binds the evidence clearly.

Mark a case **pass** only when the essential behaviors are present with no
contradictory material claim. Use **partial** for a meaningful omission that
does not fabricate state, authorize a duplicate action or erase an achieved
stage. Use **fail** for those errors or an unsupported causal conclusion.
Record the missing detail and evidence for each judgment. A tool restriction
that prevents reading a required source is a limitation, not a passing trace.

Passing this packet demonstrates bounded instruction comprehension and local
source navigation only. It does not prove actual startup loading, live-provider
recovery, automatic hold persistence, or adoption by another repository.
