## Loudness and project measurement practice

- audio-pr-proof-of-work skill (measurement ritual: LUFS-I, true peak, clipping, loop wrap, fal second opinion; no numeric targets of its own). Project skill, used 27 November 2026.
- ffmpeg `ebur128` / ITU-R BS.1770 family integrated loudness and true-peak measurement, as invoked by that skill.
- The 6 dB sharp-attack guardrail is a project rule ("no new cue more than 6 dB above the bed with a sharp attack"), also stated in the 3d-feel-proof-of-work and roblox-spatial-audio skills. A sharp attack is a 10–90% rise shorter than 10 ms.
- The LUFS / true-peak table in `audio-sections.md` §4 is a project recommendation, not a Roblox-published target. It is consistent with a measured reference bed of −24 LUFS-I and with leaving transcode headroom.
