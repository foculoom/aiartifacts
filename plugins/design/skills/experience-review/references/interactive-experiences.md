# Interactive experiences

Inspect representative input, resulting state, recovery, and return/help paths. Preserve saved progress and platform conventions. Test keyboard and touch separately when possible; a canvas label alone does not establish accessible gameplay.

Review focus after dialogs, errors, loading, empty and unavailable states, and reduced motion where relevant. Use safe local fixtures for network failures and submissions. Record untested devices and incomplete journeys. Never turn synthetic data into claims about real forecasts, customers, or transactions.
