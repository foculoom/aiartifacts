# Product pages

Check the opening for audience, concrete purpose, availability, and an action whose destination matches its label. Inspect direct entry without relying on homepage context. Distinguish a preview, complete browser product, planned store edition, and verified purchase flow.

Use approved identity and assets; record unknown rights or unapproved covers rather than declaring them ready. Check useful requirements and limitations without turning the page into an internal release checklist. Do not propose checkout, email collection, or analytics unless the visitor problem warrants their operational cost.
