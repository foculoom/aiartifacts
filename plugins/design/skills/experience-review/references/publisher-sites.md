# Publisher sites

Check whether a newcomer can explain the umbrella identity, find a relevant product, distinguish available work from development, and reach help. Different product audiences do not require separate domains or businesses.

Compare prominence with current availability and the brief. Product-specific artwork can vary while navigation, status meanings, and support remain predictable. A compact catalog or flagship-first page are choices to assess, not universal templates. Keep confidential partner work outside public examples; removing a navigation link is not access control.
