# Sources

This skill credits public sources for the observation that some wording and formulas are typical of current LLM output, and for the limit that those signs are not proof of authorship. The banned-word list, the linter, and the rewrite examples are original to this repository.

- Wikipedia contributors, "Signs of AI writing," *Wikipedia*, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing (verified 2026-11-26). Volunteer field guide to recurring wording and formulas. Descriptive, not a detector. The page is available under the licenses stated on Wikipedia (Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike and GFDL). This skill paraphrases and links; it does not copy the page.
- NPR, "Wikipedia editors publish new guide to help readers detect entries written by AI," 4 September 2025, https://www.npr.org/2025/09/04/nx-s1-5519267/wikipedia-editors-publish-new-guide-to-help-readers-detect-entries-written-by-ai (verified 2026-11-26).
- GOV.UK, "Writing for GOV.UK," https://www.gov.uk/guidance/content-design/writing-for-gov-uk (verified 2026-11-26; redirects to the current GOV.UK writing-standards page). Public guidance on plain, specific wording for government pages; useful when rewriting marketing padding, not a style the skill imposes on every brand.

Do not treat a citation here as an endorsement, and do not import later revisions of those pages without a fresh review.
